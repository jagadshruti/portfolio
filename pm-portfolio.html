AI-Powered Document Comparison and Summarization at Scale

Built and scaled AI-powered comparison and summarization workflows processing 8.6M+ monthly comparisons across desktop and cloud.

Approach

Outcome

Improved AI summary accuracy from 85% to 92%. The AI comparison engine now processes 8.6M+ monthly comparisons reliably, becoming a core differentiator and key driver of enterprise adoption.

Problem

Legal teams rely on Litera's comparison engine for best-in-class redlines, but the output was trapped in formats AI tools couldn't consume. As firms rapidly adopted LLMs, they had no way to connect Litera's comparisons to their AI workflows — and a competitor had already shipped an AI-friendly export.

Approach

Rather than building a proprietary LLM or betting on a single vendor, we took a platform-neutral approach. Added a lightweight export option that converts redline results into a clean, structured format any AI tool can read — no special integration needed.

Outcome

Repositioned Litera from a standalone comparison tool to the foundation of a firm's AI-powered review workflow — without locking customers into any single AI platform. Delivered a defensible moat: firms choose Litera for comparison quality, and the AI-Ready export ensures that value flows into every downstream tool.

Bulk Analysis Agent: From Weekend Reviews to Minutes

Built a Bulk Analysis Agent that processes dozens of document comparisons at scale and delivers structured, severity-ranked findings across key legal workflows.

Bulk Analysis Agent — structured findings across 5 legal workflows

⚡ Cross-document pattern: Indemnity cap removal appears in 8 of 52 leases — likely a systematic counterparty position.

Problem

In high-volume scenarios like due diligence, legal teams were running comparisons one by one and manually synthesizing findings across 50+ document pairs. This took entire weekends with no consistent way to spot cross-document patterns.

Approach

Designed an agent layer on top of the AI-Ready Redline export. Users feed in multiple document pairs, and the agent processes all comparisons and renders findings in an interactive table organized across five legal workflows: client memos, negotiation response, risk matrix, obligations tracking, and playbook compliance.

Outcome

Compressed what previously took a legal team an entire weekend into structured, severity-ranked output delivered in minutes. Enabled cross-document pattern detection that was practically impossible in manual workflows.

07 Negotiation AI

Smart Redlining Assistant: AI-Powered Contract Negotiation

Conceptualized an AI assistant that accelerates contract negotiation by suggesting redlines based on company playbooks and past negotiations.

Smart Redlining Assistant — AI-suggested clause changes with human override
Contract Review 🤖 AI Assistant ON Playbook: Standard NDA v3
INCOMING CONTRACT — Vendor Services Agreement
§ 2.1 Scope of Services — The Vendor shall provide services as described in Exhibit A...
✓ Matches playbook
§ 4.2 Limitation of Liability — Vendor's liability shall not exceed fees paid in the prior 3 months...
⚠ Deviates from playbook — your standard is 12 months
AI Suggestion Redline to: "...fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months..."
Based on: Playbook §4.2 · Accepted in 89% of past negotiations
✓ Accept ✎ Modify ✕ Override
§ 6.1 Termination — Either party may terminate with 15 days written notice...
⚡ Below threshold — your minimum is 30 days
AI Suggestion Primary: Redline to 30 days (playbook standard)
Fallback: Accept 15 days if mutual + cure period added
Fallback used in 3 of last 10 similar negotiations
3 clauses reviewed · 1 match · 1 high-risk · 1 medium-risk
Human override at every step →

Problem

Contract negotiation across legal teams is slow, repetitive, and inconsistent. Lawyers manually redline every incoming contract against internal playbooks, often re-doing work done dozens of times before.

Approach

Designed a POC for a Smart Redlining Assistant that analyzes incoming contracts against the firm's playbook and historical negotiation patterns. It suggests redlines and generates fallback clause options ranked by past acceptance rates — with human override at every step.

Outcome

Early validation showed significant potential to reduce first-pass review time from hours to minutes. Consistency of clause language improved by grounding every suggestion in the firm's own playbook. The concept directly extends Litera's comparison strength into the high-value negotiation workflow.

Philosophy

How I think about product

Principles that shape how I make decisions, lead squads, and build products that last.

Start with the workflow, not the feature

Features fail when they ignore the workflow they're embedded in. I map operational reality before defining solutions — especially in integration-heavy, compliance-sensitive domains like legal tech and finance.

Instrument before you assume

Pendo, Datadog, Mixpanel — the tool matters less than the habit. I instrument early, build feedback loops with real users, and let data arbitrate debates so the team ships with conviction.

Experimentation is a culture, not a step

A/B tests, targeted cohorts, and rapid validation cycles aren't add-ons — they're how my squads operate. This discipline is how we achieved 30% adoption lifts and killed features that looked good on paper.

Mentor the craft, not just the task

I invest in coaching PMs and analysts on discovery, prioritization, and experimentation rigor. Strong product teams outlast any single roadmap.
